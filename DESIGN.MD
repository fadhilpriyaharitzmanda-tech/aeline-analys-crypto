Design Specification: Aeline AI & Consulting Website

1. Overview & Aesthetic Direction

Brand Name: Aeline

Aesthetic Style: Modern SaaS / AI Enterprise, clean, futuristic yet corporate, leveraging vibrant color contrasts against natural sky imagery.

Tone: Professional, innovative, trustworthy, and high-tech.

2. Color Palette

Primary Background / Brand Color: Vibrant Sky Blue (#1e88e5 / gradient blend with clouds)

Accent / Call-to-Action (CTA): Neon Lime / Chartreuse (#ccff00 or #b8ff00)

Secondary / Supporting Elements:

Pure White (#ffffff) for cards and primary typography.

Deep Black / Dark Charcoal (#121212) for high-contrast cards and secondary text elements.

Muted Gray (#f4f5f7) for light card backgrounds.

Text Colors: White (#ffffff), Dark Charcoal (#1a1a1a), and Muted Gray (#666666).

3. Typography

Primary Font Family: Clean, geometric sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, or SF Pro Display).

Headings: Bold, large-scale, tightly kerned display font.

Hero Title: ~56px - 64px, Bold, White.

Section Titles: ~40px - 48px, Bold, Charcoal/Black.

Body Text: Regular weight, highly readable.

Hero Subtitle: ~18px - 20px, Light Blue/White.

Body Copy: ~15px - 16px, Dark Gray.

4. Page Structure & Component Breakdown

A. Navigation Bar (Header)

Logo: Left-aligned, custom geometric icon with "Aeline" text.

Menu Links: Center-aligned (HOME, SERVICES, ABOUT US, MORE LINKS ▾).

Action Button: Right-aligned pill button (BUY TEMPLATE) in solid accent lime with dark text.

B. Hero Section

Background: Bright blue sky with volumetric white clouds gradient.

Headline: "Building the future with AI and strategy"

Subheadline: "We help organizations unlock growth and efficiency through data-driven consulting and intelligent automation."

Primary CTAs: Two pill buttons (VIEW DEMO with border, and solid GET STARTED with arrow icon in lime).

Interactive/3D Element: A curved carousel showcase of floating UI cards displaying metrics, data charts, and professional headshots.

Social Proof: Rated 4.9/5 by 4,900+ clients accompanied by a 5-star yellow rating visual.

C. Client / Partner Logo Strip

Grayscale logo placeholders (Logotipsum) laid out horizontally in a clean white band separating the sections.

D. About Us / Value Proposition Section

Subheading Pill: • ABOUT US

Main Heading: "A global consulting partner dedicated to building [Icon: Blue Circle] smarter and [Icon: Lime Bulb] more adaptive"

Grid Layout (Bento-style Cards):

Card 1 (Blue Gradient): Features a person's portrait, bold metric 120+, and description text ("Collaborating with leading AI and cloud technology providers.").

Card 2 (Light Gray): Metric 100% commitment to measurable results, client avatars, and testimonial quote ("Their automation strategy completely reshaped how we work...").

Card 3 (Lime Green): Large bold metric 520k+ Data Points analyzed monthly.

Card 4 (Dark Charcoal): Metric 20+ Continents.

5. UI/UX Interaction Notes

Hover Effects: Pill buttons feature smooth scaling or color transitions; cards have subtle drop shadows that elevate on hover.

Responsiveness: Grid layouts collapse into single-column vertical stacks on mobile viewports.
